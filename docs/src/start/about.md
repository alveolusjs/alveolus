# About

## The source

The domain model and strategic design rules of Alveolus build on _Implementing Domain-Driven Design_ by Vaughn Vernon (Addison-Wesley, 2013), together with hands-on practice of NestJS and TypeScript. The architecture and placement rules come from that practice and from the default choices of Alveolus.

The rules are rephrased: no passage of the book is reproduced. They are reorganized by theme, adapted to TypeScript and NestJS, and extended with default choices specific to Alveolus (see the [default choices](./default-choices)).

For depth on the substance, the book remains the reference. This site does not aim to replace it.

## How the rules evolve

A rule has a stable code: it is never reused for anything else. A new theme is first announced as *coming*, with its prefix reserved, and appears in the navigation with its first rule. A new decision shows up as an **Our choice** paragraph in the rules concerned, and as a row in the default choices.
