# Epic: monthly statements

## Goal

Give each customer a monthly statement of each of their wallets, so that they can check their money and keep a record.

## What the customer gets

- On the first day of each month, one statement per wallet for the month before: the balance at the start and at the end of the month, and every deposit and withdrawal with its date and amount.
- The statement arrives by email as a PDF.
- The customer can list and download their past statements in the app.
- A wallet closed during the month gets a last statement.

## Out of scope

Statements on demand for any period. Paper statements.
