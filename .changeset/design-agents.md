Nine design agents, installed with the others: analyst, explorer, strategist, modeler, integrator, architect, tester, operator and critic. Each decides one part of a design and reads only the rules of that part. They write their notes in a shared work folder, and `alveolus-claude design assemble <work folder> <design>` puts them together in the order of the template, renumbering their local identifiers across the design.
